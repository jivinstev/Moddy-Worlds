## What's new in Moddy Arena 0.1.5

- **With the default 5 waves, wave 5 is now a boss wave.** Every 5th wave (5, 10, 15, ...) is a named boss with a small escort, so a battle at the default settings ends on a boss. Each player has 3 lives in a battle. The Champions board on the prep room's back wall shows the best runs at the panel's settings (#27).
- **Breaks between waves:** each wave allows 5 hotbar wishes. Between waves there is a break: the arena mends itself, and pressing **READY** on the panel starts the next wave early (#29).
- **Creature roles and the Arena Guide:** every creature you wish for takes a battle role in a wave: Fighter, Tank or Healer. The Arena Guide book is given on your first arrival and moved into your bag, and `/moddy guide` gives it again (#30).
