Third-party datapacks the Moddy Scarcity Isle level jar carries
================================================================

These packs are NOT part of Moddy. They are BluePsychoRanger's SkyBlock Collection
(https://github.com/BPR02/SkyBlock_Collection), used unmodified, downloaded by the build at the
versions and sha1s pinned in datapacks.lock.json, and shipped inside the jar under datapacks/<name>/
as built-in packs that are off unless a world is created with World Type "Moddy Isle". Each pack
folder also carries its own LICENSE file, which is the text below. The starter island structure
(data/moddingfromamod/structure/isle/starter_island.nbt) is copied from the Standard SkyBlock pack
under the same licence.

  * SkyBlock - Standard (Standard SkyBlock) 2.1.1 — by BluePsychoRanger (BPR02)
      https://modrinth.com/datapack/standard-skyblock
  * Sky Void Additions 1.4.1 — by BluePsychoRanger (BPR02)
      https://modrinth.com/datapack/sky-void-additions
  * SkyBlock Advancements 1.0.9 — credited in the pack to EpyonProjects; its LICENSE names BPR02
      https://modrinth.com/datapack/skyblock-advancements
